01 / THE PROBLEM

Students are searching everywhere.

Students want to work while studying, but finding reliable opportunities and the right skills can take too much time.

⌛

Too Much Time Searching

Students move between many websites and platforms to find a single suitable opportunity.

◌

Information Overload

Too many sources can create distraction, confusion and decision fatigue.

⚠

Skills Gap

Some students discover opportunities but do not yet have the skills required to apply confidently.

◷

Study Pressure

Without planning, work can consume the time students need for lessons, assignments and revision.

↯

Scattered Platforms

Learning and work opportunities often live in separate places, making the journey harder.

?

Unclear Direction

Students may not know which skills to learn first or which opportunities match their field.
